# Playbook: physical consumer products and hardware

- **Sources:** manufacturer manuals and specs, verified-owner reports, independent bench tests, replacement-parts catalogs, repair manuals (e.g. iFixit), warranty and recall notices, engineering constraints.
- Check certification, electrical/fire/mechanical hazards, warranty impact, part sourcing and tools needed. Never assert physical compatibility from product names alone.
- Separate a repairability/UX gap from a manufacturing defect. Look for a reversible fix first.
