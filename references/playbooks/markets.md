# Playbook: markets, services and new businesses

- **Sources:** first-hand customer complaints, credible industry publications, pricing pages, substitutes, competitors' actual product, visible demand signals, operator constraints.
- Separate a publicly stated wish from willingness to pay or change behavior.
- Look for customers already using awkward workarounds — the strongest demand signal.
- Compare distribution cost, willingness to pay, operating burden, regulation, competitor speed and defensibility.
- Prefer interviews, landing-page demand tests or small paid pilots over invented market-size estimates.
