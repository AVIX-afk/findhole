# Playbook: manufacturing and industrial processes

- **Sources:** operating records, scrap/yield trends, OEM documentation, technical papers, safety data sheets, relevant standards and regulations, material specifications, comparable processes.
- Turn anecdotes into a measurable baseline: input, output, yield, contamination/spec risk, capital cost, uptime/downtime, quality acceptance.
- Food-contact polymers: prioritize documented specs, food-contact regulations, barrier properties, compatibility, material history and qualified process-engineering review before any trial or reuse.
- Alternatives include segregation, process control, mechanical recycling with compatibilization, downcycling, third-party recycling, or avoiding waste at the source. Never present a lab demonstration as production-ready.
- Test plans need authorized plant oversight, safety review, defined sampling and go/no-go thresholds. Do not change production settings directly.
